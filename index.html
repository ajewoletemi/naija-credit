Terms & Conditions

Last Updated: November 2026

These Terms govern the use of the NaijaLink Credit demonstration platform. By proceeding you acknowledge that you have read and understood them.

1. Nature of Service

NaijaLink Credit is presented as a demonstration of a digital credit application flow designed for the Nigerian market. It illustrates typical features of virtual credit products including application, assessment simulation, and virtual card presentation.

2. Eligibility

  • You must be at least 18 years of age and a resident of Nigeria.
  • You must provide accurate personal and financial information.
  • Possession of a valid BVN and/or NIN is required for identity verification in regulated environments.

3. Credit Assessment & Offer

  • Any credit limit shown is the result of a simulated assessment based on the data you enter.
  • In a regulated product, the lender must assess capacity to repay before granting credit (FCCPC DEON Regulations 2025).
  • Credit is provided only on an explicit opt-in basis. Automatic or pre-authorised lending is not permitted under Nigerian digital lending rules.

4. Pricing & Transparency

  • Interest rates, fees, total cost of credit and repayment schedule must be clearly disclosed before acceptance.
  • Sample daily interest illustration used in this demo: up to 0.08% per day (indicative maximum APR illustration ≈ 29.2%). Actual rates in a live product would be set by the licensed lender and fully disclosed.
  • No hidden charges. All applicable fees must be shown before you confirm.

5. Data Protection

  • Personal data is handled in accordance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation.
  • Data collected is used only for the purpose of the demonstration and is stored locally in your browser (localStorage) for the session.
  • In a live regulated service, BVN/NIN verification, credit bureau checks, and data sharing would require explicit consent and lawful basis.

6. Debt Recovery

Any real lending product operating in Nigeria must recover debts only through lawful means. Harassment, public shaming, contacting third parties from a borrower’s phone book, or other prohibited practices are not allowed under FCCPC rules.

7. Complaints

In a live service, complaints must be acknowledged within 24 hours and resolved within the timelines prescribed by the FCCPC (generally 24–48 hours where practicable). Unresolved complaints may be escalated to the FCCPC.

8. Regulatory Context

Digital consumer lending in Nigeria is regulated by the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Commission (FCCPC) under the DEON Regulations 2025, state Money Lenders Laws, and, where applicable, the Central Bank of Nigeria. A live product requires the appropriate licences and FCCPC approval of the lending application/platform.

9. Limitation

This demonstration does not create any lender–borrower relationship and does not constitute an offer of credit by any licensed financial institution.
